# Don't

The things that make a screen look like every other generated UI. Remove them on sight.

## Surfaces
- A white card with a shadow around every list item or every section.
- Cards inside cards.
- A coloured left border on a card to show status.
- Glassmorphism, blur panels, glow, neon.

## Colour
- Gradients of any kind, including "subtle" ones on buttons and headers.
- Blue or purple as an accent. The accent is ink, and `--brand` green for links.
- Colour used for decoration: a mint header bar, a lilac section background.
- Red for errors or alerts. Use `--attention` (clay) on `--attention-soft`.
- Category colours on text outside a tile or a chart.

## Type
- More than one font family.
- Bold everywhere. Body is 500, names 600, headings 700, hero figures 300.
- A hero figure in bold.
- Proportional digits in tables or totals.

## Components
- More than one filled button on a screen.
- Icon-only buttons without a label or `aria-label`.
- Emoji as icons or in headings.
- Stat cards in a 4-column grid as the top of a dashboard. Use one hero figure and a River or a ledger.
- Avatars or brand logos as list markers. Use a **Tile** (letter on a soft ground).
- Pie and donut charts. Use a horizontal stacked bar or the River.

## Copy
- "Welcome back, John! 👋" headers.
- Exclamation marks.
- Placeholder Latin text in examples; write real, short content.
